# UX review and changes, 12 November 2026

What was looked at, what was found, what was changed, and what is still open.
This file describes its date. The plan it leaves behind is in
[ROADMAP.md](../ROADMAP.md); the completed changes are in
[CHANGELOG.md](../CHANGELOG.md).

## Why

The owner's direction for this pass: the site should work for people, not
only look good; Bitbaum should become a platform for builders, not only a
studio; shared packages are used, and changed where they fall short.

## How it was looked at

- Every page was opened in a real browser at 390px and 1440px, light and dark,
  and read one screen at a time.
- The eight journeys the site exists for were walked with the network mocked,
  so nothing was sent to the studio: commissioning, applying as a builder,
  hiring a builder, building with Loki, the course, coming back with and
  without a link, finding a package, replying from a project page.
- The generator, the stylesheet and all fifteen gates were read, so a change
  would move the gates with it rather than around them.
- Loki's studio routes, accountkit, sitekit and the shared sign-in service were
  read for what a static site can honestly offer as "sign in".

## A second look: what an adversarial review found

Once both changes were merged, three reviewers were told to break them, one
each for behaviour, layout and accessibility, and truth. Every serious finding
went to a second reviewer told to refute it. Ten held and three were refuted,
one of those only because the text was older than these changes. The rest were
small. All of it is fixed in the third change of the day. The table has ten
rows: the two findings about `/hire/` share one, and the refuted `/solon/`
finding has one, because it is fixed all the same.

| Found | Measured | Now |
|---|---|---|
| "Request this builder" landed at the top of `/hire/` | "Asking for …" 5,439px down at 390px; the first change's own scroll fix caused it | arrives at the box, which names the builder; no cursor is put in it |
| The first message pushed the box and the receipt under the fold | the page moved up 284px at 390px and 312px at 1440px, cursor still in the box | both stay on screen, after a failed send as well |
| A link to the box put the cursor in a box that was off the screen | box 1,834px down on an 844px screen, focused | the box takes the cursor only when it is on screen |
| "Opens at the top" passed when the chat never loaded | the timeout was swallowed | it fails and says the check did not run |
| `/builders/`: "you publish a profile … shown when you say so" | in Loki the builder proposes and the studio publishes | the page says that |
| `/hire/`: a reply "in your private portal", and "the only email" is the slot notice | a first message with no website gets no portal; Loki mails the private link | both lines say what happens |
| "A studio and its builders" in the footer, the description and the share picture | no builder is listed | "A studio in Zürich, and a place for builders who work with agents." |
| `/solon/` and `/loki/` still said fixed "red lines" | the map had already been corrected | both state Solon's current rule |
| Links on `/studio/` ran off the screen at 200% text | 31px at 390px, 101px at 320px | they wrap |
| The "what happens next" lists broke words letter by letter at 200% text | the sentence column was 35px wide at 320px | the label stands above its sentence when the list is narrow |

Settling the last two meant opening every page at 200% text. That found the
same kind of defect where no reviewer had looked: `/studio/`, the home page and
eight project pages wider than the screen, and badges and prices cut off by
their cards on `/hire/`, `/map/` and `/work/`. Every page is now clean at
320px and 390px, the geometry of every card title row is unchanged at the
normal size (652 boxes compared before and after), and
`site/check-responsive.mjs` checks every page at both widths.

One fix made a new defect, caught by that same scan before it shipped: a rule
that showed the script-less menu link also printed its markup as text in the
header when scripts were on. It applies only where scripts are off.

## A third look: the fixes themselves, reviewed before they were opened

The second look found its defects after the changes were already published.
So the third change was pushed to its branch, reviewed the same way, and
opened for merge only after that. Nine findings held, and every one was made
by the fixes: six in what a visitor sees, three in the new checks.

| Found in the fixes | Measured | Now |
|---|---|---|
| Keeping the box on screen pulled back a reader who had scrolled away | scroll up 350px while a reply is pending: pulled 493px down at 390px, 575px at 1440px, when the reply arrived | a wheel, a finger, a scroll key or the scrollbar means the reader left; they are left where they are until they send again or put the cursor back in the box |
| "The cursor only when the box is on screen" was asked while the page was still gliding | on a desk all five links to the box on `/hire/` left it without the cursor; at 367ms the box was still 1,000px below the screen | asked once the page has come to rest; a box partly in sight gets the cursor and is brought fully in |
| A refused file started a conversation | "Asking for …" vanished, the greeting became a turn, the box said "or say send it" | the reason stands beside the box and nothing else changes |
| A refused file stayed attached | the next message sent it again and it was refused twice | the box lets go of it |
| One button size on phones was the larger one | ten labels that fitted on one line wrapped at 320px to 414px, and a wrapped label touched its border | the smaller size, with room above and below; no label takes more lines than on the published site |
| Links on `/studio/` split across lines at the normal size | "origin / register", "Software / Heritage" at five widths | a link moves to the next line whole, and breaks only when wider than its column |
| The contrast check's new step measured a failure message on `/builders/` and no receipt on `/hire/` | its mock did not match what the box posts | both saves are answered, and the check fails if no reply or no receipt appears |
| The large-text check missed text cut off inside the screen | "Four to six weeks" ran 25px past its card and stopped 15px short of the screen's edge | text is also compared with the nearest box that hides what overflows it |
| The large-text check measured before the chat had loaded | 0 composers at measure time on `/hire/`, `/builders/`, `/loki/` | it waits for the box, and fails if it never comes |

Smaller things from the same review, also fixed: the small chat on the home
page and every project page had the same "box under the fold" defect as the
contact box and now shares its fix; a builder link with no name said nothing
about who was asked for; a directory entry with a missing name or rate printed
"undefined"; the focus ring after "Check again" was drawn round the whole
directory; and the box offered "the reply by email" for a request that is
never answered by mail.

What two rounds say about method: a fix to how a page moves or lays out is
itself a change that can break, and reading it is not enough. Each of the six
visitor-facing defects above was found by running the page, and none by the
gates as they then stood.

## What was changed in other repositories

- **chatkit**: three changes. [#84](https://github.com/bitbaum/chatkit/pull/84)
  (merged) is the cause of the scroll: a thread holding only a greeting, on a
  page, no longer takes the page to its own end. [#86](https://github.com/bitbaum/chatkit/pull/86)
  (merged) gives the composer a visible border while it has keyboard focus;
  it measured 1.2:1 to 1.5:1 against its background. The third ([#88](https://github.com/bitbaum/chatkit/pull/88), merged) is the
  cause of the box leaving the screen: following never scrolls a page up, and
  a host can keep room under the thread with `--ck-follow-gap`. Each has a
  browser test that fails without the change.
- **Loki** ([#1672](https://github.com/bitbaum/loki/pull/1672), open): the
  backend half of signing in from this site. `GET /api/studio-session` tells
  this origin who is looking (a name, a handle, a picture, whether they run the
  studio, their builder applications by status; never an email, an id or a
  key), and `/studio/return?to=/builders/` signs a person in and brings them
  back to the page they were on.
- **accountkit** ([#92](https://github.com/bitbaum/accountkit/pull/92), merged):
  one account control for signed out, loading and signed in; a reader for the
  session summary that never throws; and a menu that stays on the screen
  wherever it is placed.
- **sitekit** ([#51](https://github.com/bitbaum/sitekit/pull/51), merged): the
  first three of the nine changes this site needs before it can take its
  header and footer from the package: its own box model, links to other
  origins, a footer year that does not read the clock.

## What is still open

In the order it should be done. Each has its line in ROADMAP.md.

1. **Sign in from the header.** It needs Loki #1672 deployed and accountkit's
   account control released. Until then the only sign-in is "Builder sign-in",
   which leads to Loki's page for an approved builder who connected an account.
2. **A builder workspace opened by an account.** Today a signed-in builder can
   only have a new private link made, which ends the old one. Loki needs
   routes that authorise by account instead of by link.
3. **The first profile in the directory.** It is empty. The page says so.
4. **Header, menu and footer from sitekit.** Measured against this site, sitekit
   needed nine changes first. The three that were defects for its current
   adopters are merged (sitekit #51); six remain. `package-plan.json` records
   why the chrome is still local.
5. **Text over drifting clouds.** The painted clouds are part of the sky and pass behind running text. The contrast check now covers the sky's own colours, not a cloud that happens to be behind a line. A reading surface behind text sections, or cloud-free bands, is the next step, and it changes how the site looks.
6. **Two kinds of receipt for one request.** A first message with no website
   address becomes a note with a Loki tracking link, not a portal; if an
   address comes later the portal link replaces it. A founder describing a new
   product therefore gets no portal and no "Lost the link?". This needs a
   request kind on Loki for an idea without a website (already on the roadmap).
7. **The private link lives only in the tab.** It is kept in session storage and
   taken out of the address bar, so a new tab or a bookmark loses the request.
   Keeping it longer is a decision about shared computers, and it is the
   owner's.
8. **The approved builder's portal is one long page.** Eight sections, 5.2
   screens on a phone, three primary buttons on one preview screen.
9. **Package pages.** The install command sits below the first screen and can
   lag the registry, because the version comes from Fleet's package register.
10. **Search.** `/work/` and `/packages/` filter by substring. Pull request
   [#297](https://github.com/bitbaum/bitbaum/pull/297) moves both to searchkit
   and is waiting on the package rollout gate.
11. **Weight.** The home page loads 1.3 MB before it is ready, 726 KB of it
    nine screenshots at 1280×800 shown about 330px wide. A smaller size per
    screenshot would more than halve it.
12. **The stylesheet.** 56 distinct font sizes, 13 breakpoints and about 8 KB
    that no page uses. This pass unified the buttons and fixed alignment; the
    type scale is untouched.
13. **One-word card titles at twice the text size.** At 200% text on a 320px
    screen a title such as "Diplodoctor" or "design-tokens" still breaks
    mid-word, because the card's padding grows with the text. Nothing is cut
    off and no page is wider than the screen.
14. **The page still moves twice per message.** Up about 150px on send, and
    down 150px to 250px when the reply lands, because the installed chat
    package still follows upward. Taking the release with chatkit #88 removes the
    first move, and the local correction with it.

## Decisions that are the owner's

- **How far up the builder moves.** The builder now has a door on the first
  screen, but `site/PURPOSE.md` still orders the readers with the person who
  commissions work first, and the headline is still "One trunk. Many
  products."
- **The name on the sign-in button** once the header has one: "Sign in", or
  "Sign in with OrangeCat". The portal, the private page and Loki's own
  sign-in page use three different names today.
- **Whether a request may be kept on a device** beyond the tab it was made in.
- **What "auto" means for the theme.** It follows the sun where the reader is, not the device's light or dark setting; a reader with a dark device sees a light page at noon.
- **How work reaches an approved builder**: requests from the directory, briefs the studio passes on, or both. The builders page cannot describe it until it is decided, and says only that nobody is promised work.
- **The headline.** Two alternatives came out of the content review: "A home for builders who work with agents." and "Software built with agents. Judged by people."
